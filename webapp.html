About

About UnboxdNFT

UnboxdNFT is a platform that allows users to open digital cases containing NFTs of varying rarity. The platform uses blockchain technology to ensure transparency and authenticity of all transactions and NFT ownership.

How it Works

1. Purchase a case using ETH or UCoins
2. Open the case to reveal your NFT
3. Keep your NFT in your collection or sell it on the marketplace

Rarity Levels

  • Legendary - Extremely rare and valuable NFTs (1% chance)
  • Epic - Very rare NFTs with special attributes (5% chance)
  • Rare - Uncommon NFTs with unique features (15% chance)
  • Common - Standard NFTs that are more widely available (79% chance)

Contact Support

Need help or have questions? Contact our support team through the Telegram bot. We're available 24/7 to assist with any issues or inquiries about the platform.
