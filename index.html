About Labubu NFTs

Collect unique digital Labubu characters with different rarities and themes. Each NFT is a one-of-a-kind collectible with varying drop rates.

Rarity System

  • Common (Tier 1) - Basic Labubu designs
  • Rare (Tier 2) - Enhanced themed versions
  • Epic (Tier 3) - Special event Labubus
  • Legendary (Tier 4) - Powerful rare variants
  • Mythic (Tier 5) - Extremely rare designs
  • Divine (Tier 6) - Ultimate collector items
